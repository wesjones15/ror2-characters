# Olimar

Captain Olimar and his Pikmin from **Pikmin** as a Risk of Rain 2 survivor: pluck, throw, whistle and lead a squad that carries enemies home to the Onion, plus **Ship Repair**, a mode where you rebuild the Dolphin from 30 parts.

Requires your own copy of Pikmin (GameCube). Nothing from the game is included in this package.

Extras:
- Louie, Pikmin 2's enemies, treasures and Candypop Buds, with your own copy of Pikmin 2.
- A Captain Pikachu skin, with your own copy of Super Smash Bros. Melee.
- With **wesleyasaurus-Mario** installed, a Pianta skin; Mario can also join Ship Repair.

## Setup

Install with r2modman, then follow the setup guide: https://github.com/wesjones15/ror2-characters/blob/modern/1.5.0/docs/Install.md

Used the GitHub download before (Olimar 0.5.x)? Delete the old `BepInEx/plugins/OlimarTest` folder from your Risk of Rain 2 folder so it doesn't load beside this one. The guide's "Upgrading" section has the rest.

## Notes

- Offline and friends-only co-op. Don't use it in public lobbies.
- Makes no internet connections; multiplayer uses Risk of Rain 2's own connection.
- Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Nintendo, Hopoo Games or Gearbox. Source, guides and changelogs: https://github.com/wesjones15/ror2-characters
