# Olimar

Play Risk of Rain 2 as **Captain Olimar**, leading a squad of Pikmin. You don't shoot: you throw Pikmin at enemies, whistle them back, and let your army do the fighting. Olimar and his Pikmin run on a reimplementation of Pikmin's own game logic, so they move, latch on, carry and fight the way they do in Pikmin.

Requires your own copy of **Pikmin** (GameCube).

## How he plays

You start each run with **10 Red, 5 Yellow and 5 Blue Pikmin**. Your ship, the S.S. Dolphin, lands behind you, and your squad climbs out of the Onion, which then lifts off and follows you through the stage.

- **Throw.** Toss Pikmin at an enemy and they latch onto its body and attack. Throws reach twice as far as in Pikmin and steer onto your cursor. Lock onto an enemy to throw rapidly at it. Pikmin hits count as your damage, so crits and on-hit items work through your squad.
- **Whistle and swarm.** Whistle to call stragglers back, swarm your squad toward a target, and pick which colour you throw next.
- **Dismiss strike.** A melee blow all around Olimar that staggers nearby enemies and sends your squad charging in. A kill refunds its cooldown.
- **Grow your army.** Dead enemies leave pellets. Your Pikmin carry them home to the Onion, which turns them into seeds. Pluck the sprouts to add Pikmin to your squad. Pikmin grow from leaf to bud to flower as they fight.
- **Colours matter.** Reds are immune to fire, Yellows hit flying and mechanical enemies harder, and Blues are tougher in water. Purples (heavy, ground-pounding, ten times as strong at carrying) and Whites (fast and poisonous) join with your copy of Pikmin 2, which also adds Candypop Buds that turn the Pikmin you throw in into more Pikmin.
- **Bring down fliers.** Enough Pikmin hanging on a flying enemy drag it to the ground.
- **Keep them alive.** Enemy attacks hurt the Pikmin near where they land, so position matters. Pikmin regenerate over time, and your regeneration items help them too. Pikmin that fall off the map come back to you.
- **The Onion.** One Onion stores all your colours. Stand in its beam to store or release Pikmin, sacrifice stored Pikmin to heal, recall stuck carriers, or send it to pluck sprouts for you.
- **Onion Ride.** Your escape tool: board the Onion and fly it across the map at twice your walking speed, with a boost. While aboard, the Onion takes the hits, not you. You can call it to you from anywhere.

Your squad and the Onion's stock carry over between stages.

## Ship Repair mode

A separate game mode for solo or co-op: start with your ship wrecked and **recover 30 parts** to rebuild it. Parts are scattered across stages and dropped by powerful enemies; your Pikmin carry each one back to the Dolphin and it gets fitted with the original repair animations. Race your friends to finish first, or turn on co-op to repair together.

Pikmin's own creatures join the stage's monsters: Bulborbs, Wollywogs, the Cannon Beetle, Puffstools, Smoky Progg and more. With Pikmin 2, its creatures and bosses join too, and its treasures are scattered around as bonus pickups. Stage music comes from Pikmin and reacts when enemies are near.

## Skins

- **Louie**, with his own squad of Purple and White Pikmin and his ship (with Pikmin 2). Unlocked by recovering all 30 parts in one Ship Repair run.
- **Captain Pikachu**, with Ice Climbers as his Pikmin (with Super Smash Bros. Melee). Unlocked after stage 2's teleporter as Olimar.
- **Pianta**, from Super Mario Sunshine (with **wesleyasaurus-Mario** installed). Unlocked after stage 1's teleporter as Olimar.

With **wesleyasaurus-Mario** installed, Mario can join Ship Repair too and carry parts by hand.

## Controls

Plays on a gamepad (Pikmin's layout: A throws, RT + B whistles, the right stick swarms, LT locks on) or keyboard and mouse (your RoR2 binds). The full control list is in the guide.
