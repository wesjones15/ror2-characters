# Mario 0.4.6, Olimar 0.4.2 and Melee 0.4.5

A Melee update: redrawn skill icons for all eight fighters, Link's bow and hookshot, and effect fixes. Mario and Olimar are unchanged from `release-20261027` and keep their versions. These notes list changes since `release-20261027`.

## Mario 0.4.6

- No changes.

## Olimar 0.4.2

- No changes.

## Melee 0.4.5

- **Every fighter's skill icon is redone.** Each shows the real attack mid-animation, drawn from the game's own model with its effects and held items, on its own background colour. Every fighter's Shield skill shows the same shield bubble.
- **Link's Hero's Bow** is drawn in his hand with its own animations, and he can walk or sprint while drawing it.
- **Link's hookshot.** Grab fires a 15 m chain that pulls him to walls, bosses and large enemies, and reels in the rest for the usual pummel and throw.
- **Luigi's Super Scope charge** uses the disc's own flash, ground smoke, recoil dust and camera kick.
- **Mirrored particle sprites draw correctly**, effect models start their own dust and sparks, and ground dust sits on the floor.
- **Falloff recovery effects** play on the returning fighter. **Egg Lay** plays its grab sound on every lash. **Falcon's Kick flame** follows his foot without turning with the leg.

**Samus, Fox, Luigi and Yoshi remain the most complete Melee fighters.** Pikachu, Captain Falcon, Link and Donkey Kong are still in progress.

## Install or upgrade

1. Download **release-20261027b.zip**, unblock it in Windows Properties if needed, and extract it.
2. Merge the included `BepInEx` folder into your mod-manager profile or the folder containing `Risk of Rain 2.exe`. Replace each component's two DLLs together.
3. The supplied config files contain release defaults. Skip `BepInEx/config` to preserve your settings and disc paths; new settings are added automatically on launch.
4. Each mod extracts assets from your own disc image: Super Mario Sunshine, Pikmin, or Super Smash Bros. Melee NTSC-U revision 2. Existing caches are reused; Luigi also requires the Melee asset cache. Set `Paths.Disc` if needed. The skill icons are regenerated from the Melee cache on first launch.

Requires RoR2 DLC4 (2026), Windows x64, BepInEx 5.4.21.0 and the dependencies in the included guides. Mario uses native ABI 31, Olimar ABI 46 and Melee ABI 5. Install matching component builds for offline/friends-only co-op.

Automated/headless verification is included in the release process; these changes have not been played in RoR2. No disc images, extracted game assets, game DLLs or dependency DLLs are included. The ZIP includes guides, component changelog entries and a source/hash manifest, with a separate SHA-256 checksum.
