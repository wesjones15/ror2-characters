# Mario 0.5.5, Olimar 0.5.4 and Melee 0.4.11 - 2026-11-05

One ZIP with all three mods, for Risk of Rain 2 **DLC4 (2026)** with BepInEx 5.4.21.0 and RoR2BepInExPack
1.46.0 already installed. Offline and friends-only co-op only.
**Everyone in a multiplayer game needs these builds.** Install each `.dll` with its native library from the same ZIP.

| Mod | Version | Folder in `BepInEx/plugins` | Your disc |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mario | **0.5.5** | `MarioTest` (`MarioTest.dll`, `sms.dll`) | Super Mario Sunshine |
| Olimar | **0.5.4** | `OlimarTest` (`OlimarTest.dll`, `pikmin.dll`) | Pikmin (and Pikmin 2 for its content) |
| Melee | **0.4.11** | `Melee` (`Samus.dll`, `ssbm.dll`) | Super Smash Bros. Melee, NTSC-U rev 2 |

Changes since the last release (Mario 0.5.4, Olimar 0.5.3, Melee 0.4.10). This build is mostly untested in game: the VR
support and Bowser Jr. have not been played in this form.

## Mario 0.5.5 (native ABI 47)

- **Bowser Jr. and his goop.** A new Shadow Mario / Bowser Jr. survivor on Mario's sim, with Sunshine's goop world,
  goop critters (Swoopin' Stus, goop Piranhas), Petey Piranha as sidekick, screen refraction over Shadow Mario and the
  disc's scrolling paint. The goop world is mirrored in multiplayer.
- **Mario in VR (opt-in).** First person, forearms and gloves on the controllers, nozzle wheel in the right hand,
  pointer-aimed FLUDD, HUD on a wrist panel; every skin's VR arms are baked on first run.
- **Corkscrew hold** keeps going while the stick keeps turning; **Spray bursts** follow press time.
- Multiplayer: coin pickup for non-Mario players, puppets nudge instead of teleporting, silent nozzle fitting for spectators.
- Native panics from `sms.dll` are logged.

## Olimar 0.5.4 (native ABI 62)

- **Olimar in VR (opt-in).** First person for every skin, his arms on the controllers, the held Pikmin in the throwing
  hand, HUD / Onion window / Ship Repair overlays on the wrist panel, a VR pause sheet.
- **Free cursor** (aimed point kept in 3D) for the VR pointer.
- **Onion** walks, jumps and climbs the legs the host draws; the flying Onion's entry jump is an arc; its dormancy
  comes from the sim.
- **Dolphin arrival** lands in the goal spot's rings with a short dust cloud; per-particle alpha is applied.
- Jump pads take the motor's throw; Pellet Posy holds its death trigger until healthy; audio follows device changes.
- Native panics from `pikmin.dll` are logged.

## Melee 0.4.11 (native ABI 5)

- **Melee in VR (opt-in)** for every fighter through the shared MeleeVr layer, with a `[VR]` settings section and
  first-run VR arm baking for every costume.
- Common fighter behaviour moved into shared defaults; native panics from `ssbm.dll` are logged.

Full per-mod lists are in `CHANGELOG.md`.
