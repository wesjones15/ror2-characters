# Mario 0.3.7, Olimar 0.3.14 and Melee 0.3.0 (Risk of Rain 2 DLC4)

Three mods in one ZIP, for Risk of Rain 2 **DLC4 (2026)** with BepInEx 5.4.21.0 and RoR2BepInExPack 1.46.0 already installed.
Offline and friends-only co-op only. Everyone in a multiplayer game needs the same Mario and Olimar builds.

| Mod | Version | Folder in `BepInEx/plugins` | Native ABI | Your disc |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mario | 0.3.7 | `MarioTest` (`MarioTest.dll`, `sms.dll`) | 29 | Super Mario Sunshine |
| Olimar | 0.3.14 | `OlimarTest` (`OlimarTest.dll`, `pikmin.dll`) | 41 | Pikmin |
| Melee | 0.3.0 | `Melee` (`Samus.dll`, `ssbm.dll`); five of its eight fighters are a first pass | 5 | Super Smash Bros. Melee, NTSC-U rev 2 |

> **Melee is not finished.** **Samus, Fox and Luigi** are the most complete. **Pikachu, Captain Falcon, Link, Yoshi and Donkey Kong are a first pass: they are not complete and not ready.** Their attacks still have to be finished and tuned, and other per-character details are missing or rough, so expect wrong, missing or placeholder moves, hitboxes, effects and numbers. They are included so they can be tried and so feedback can shape them, not as finished characters.

## Install

1. **Before unzipping, right-click the ZIP, choose Properties and tick Unblock** (or run `Unblock-File` on it in PowerShell).
   Windows marks every file you download, and a marked ZIP marks all its contents, so copying the DLLs into the game
   would otherwise show "These files might be harmful to your computer". Clicking OK on that prompt is also safe; it is the
   standard downloaded-file notice, not a problem found in the DLLs. The DLLs in this ZIP carry no such mark themselves.
2. Merge the ZIP's `BepInEx` folder into your game folder (the one with `Risk of Rain 2.exe`), or into your mod manager's
   profile. Copy only the folders for the mods you want; install each mod's two DLLs together.
3. Keep the discs out of the ZIP: launch once, set `Paths.Disc` in each mod's config file (or put the disc image beside its
   DLLs; for Melee its filename must contain "Melee"), and launch again. Extraction runs once, on your machine.
4. The guides are in `docs/`: `Mario.md`, `Olimar.md` and `Melee.md` (install, controls, configuration, limits).

**Upgrading Samus 0.2.x? Melee replaces it and must not run beside it.** Delete `BepInEx/plugins/MeleeSamus/` and
`BepInEx/config/com.libssbm.samus.cfg`, install the new `Melee` folder, set `Paths.Disc` in the new
`com.libssbm.melee.cfg`, and move your old `MeleeSamus/assets` into `Melee/assets` (or set `Paths.AssetsDir`) to skip
re-extracting. Mario and Olimar keep their folders and configs.

## What's new

Only the changes since each mod's last published version (Mario 0.3.6, Olimar 0.3.11, Samus 0.2.17); see `CHANGELOG.md`.

**Melee 0.3.0**: one plugin with eight Smash survivors. New: **Fox and Luigi** (more complete) and a **first pass** of
**Pikachu, Captain Falcon, Link, Yoshi and Donkey Kong** (not complete or ready, see the note above) beside Samus, each with his disc costumes (unlocked by clearing stage teleporter events as him), portrait,
announcer call, intro, KO and a pause-menu controls window. **Pikachu** has Thunder Shock (a crosshair-aimed orb that bounces
ten times), Skull Bash (charge and lunge), Quick Attack (two zips that home on your lock-on, the nearest enemy to your
crosshair) and Thunder on RB. Dash attacks keep the ground they cover. Samus's intro is now her Screw Attack with the
announcer's name call, and the Screw Attack makes her invulnerable until she lands.

**Mario 0.3.7**: Mario plays **Ship Repair** with the new Olimar plugin (B by a part picks it up; he carries, puts or throws it
into his Dolphin). A reworked **corkscrew**: from a dive, or from any jump with the Turbo held, a stick circle keeps him
spinning for as long as you circle, in flames after 4 s with a 2000% super blow (`Damage.SuperCorkscrew`), and a blazing slide
on landing. A circle during the Rocket's climb is a burning **rocket spin** (1000% on enemies above). Leaving the map through
the top no longer shows TOO BAD. His controls are listed beside RoR2's pause menu. Cheaper multiplayer replay and rendering.

**Olimar 0.3.14**: Ship Repair for guests, a Main Engine that gates the Dolphin's flight to the teleporter, and **Pikmin enemies**
(Dwarf and Spotty Bulborbs from your disc, with director cards, corpse pellets and a part for a big one's killer; `[Enemies]`
config). The stutter in Olimar's run is fixed. An Onion sleeps when its Olimar dies and his Pikmin join a friend or go home.
His controls are listed beside the pause menu, and the portrait is turned and closer.

## Checks and known limits

- The libsms, libpikmin and libssbm test suites, the managed Mario, Olimar and shared-code checks, and Melee's plugin startup
  and native-boundary checks pass on these sources (`release.json` pins every source commit).
- **None of this was played in game before release.** Melee's five first-pass fighters (Pikachu, Captain Falcon, Link, Yoshi,
  Donkey Kong) are covered by headless tests against the disc and boundary checks only; see the limits in `docs/Melee.md`. Melee does not support multiplayer: a client controlling a Melee
  survivor falls back to Commando.
- The ZIP contains no disc images, extracted game assets, generated audio, game DLLs or dependency DLLs. Unofficial fan project,
  not affiliated with Nintendo, HAL Laboratory, Hopoo Games or Gearbox. Do not share your extracted `assets/` folders.
- Verify the download against the `.sha256` file on the release page; `release.json` lists the SHA-256 of every file inside.
