# Mario 0.4.7, Olimar 0.4.3 and Melee 0.4.6

Yoshi joins Mario, along with a nozzle wheel and Melee punches. Olimar gets three new skins, Purple and White Pikmin, and Candypop Buds, and Ship Repair picks up Pikmin 2's enemies and treasures. All three mods handle rough ground better, and they can now share one folder for your discs. The newest changes come first; the earlier 0.4.x notes follow below.

## New in this release (since `release-20261027b`)

### Mario 0.4.7

- **Play as Yoshi.** Defeat Mithrix's final form while playing Mario to unlock him (you don't have to be alive or land the last hit). He runs on his own legs, flutters when you press A again in the air, and sprays FLUDD's water from his mouth in his juice colour.
- **Pick your nozzle from a wheel.** Tap RB to swap between Spray and your second nozzle. Hold RB for half a second and choose that nozzle from a wheel: Hover, Rocket, Turbo, or No FLUDD. On keyboard, the Special key does the same and the mouse picks.
- **No FLUDD? Punch.** With FLUDD off, RB and RT throw Melee Mario's attacks with his voice: jab, tilts, smashes and dash attack on the ground, aerials in the air. Needs the Melee mod installed with its cache built.
- **Fire is a real threat, and water is the answer.** Catching fire sends Mario hopping with his Sunshine burn voice and flames. A pool, your own spray or another Mario's puts him out, and a wet Mario can't catch fire for a moment. FLUDD does **5x damage** to burning enemies, fire elites, wisps and Magma Worms, and spraying a fire trail puts that stretch out.
- **Sunshine's heat haze** shimmers over distant scenery (`Look.HeatHaze` turns it off).
- **Your HUD stays when you die.** Coins, life meter and tank gauge stay up while you spectate or fly a Remote Operation drone.
- **Smoother on rough ground.** No more sinking into slopes or getting stuck in rocks (a wedged Mario is freed after 2 s). He steps up small lips instead of stuttering, keeps his speed on ramps, and can start a wet slide over dry ground.

### Olimar 0.4.3

- **Three new skins.** **Pianta** unlocks after stage 1's teleporter as Olimar, **Captain Pikachu** (Ice Climbers for Pikmin) after stage 2's, and **Louie** for recovering all 30 ship parts in one Ship Repair run. Pianta uses the Mario mod's Sunshine files, Captain Pikachu Melee's, and Louie needs a Pikmin 2 disc.
- **Purple and White Pikmin.** Purples pound where they land and knock fliers out of the sky; Whites are fast and poison whatever eats them. Every Onion holds all five colours.
- **Candypop Buds on every map** (with a Pikmin 2 disc). Throw Pikmin in, and **every 5 that go in come straight back out as 5 live Pikmin of the bud's colour**, landing beside it in your squad. A bud takes 25 before it withers; the rainbow Pop gives 5 for 1. Lock onto buds like enemies.
- **Weigh fliers down.** 5 Pikmin hanging on a small flier bring it to the ground, 15 on a large one, 25 on a boss.
- **Ship Repair goes Pikmin 2** (with a Pikmin 2 disc). About thirty more enemies join the stage, from Bulborb Larvae and Snagrets to the Empress Bulblax, the Segmented Crawbster and the Long Legs. Pikmin 2 **treasures** lie around each stage for a separate treasure score, in co-op and the race.
- **Smoother on slopes.** Olimar and his Pikmin no longer hop off downhills or restart their run when sprinting over hills, and anyone wedged in geometry for 2 s is freed.

### Melee 0.4.6

- **Fighters handle rough ground better.** Jumps and knockback no longer carry them into slopes or overhangs, a stuck fighter is freed after 2 s, moves keep their full speed along slopes, and walking up lumpy slopes no longer stutters.
- **Skill icons are lit properly** instead of coming out as dark silhouettes.
- **Pikachu's Thunder** rumbles at the right moment.
- The first launch with your Melee disc also extracts Melee Mario, for the Mario mod's punches.

**Samus, Fox, Luigi and Yoshi remain the most complete Melee fighters.** Pikachu, Captain Falcon, Link and Donkey Kong are still in progress.

### All three: one folder for your discs

The ZIP now includes `BepInEx/plugins/gamecube_library.txt`. It is only notes until you write a folder's path into it. Then put your disc images in that folder, under any names: each mod finds its own game by reading the discs. A mod that hasn't extracted yet builds its files in `assets\Mario`, `assets\Olimar` or `assets\Melee` inside that folder. **Caches you already have stay where they are and keep working.** The mods remember the folder, so a later update that replaces the file keeps it.

## Install or upgrade

1. Download **release-20261029.zip**. On Windows, right-click it, open Properties and tick **Unblock** before extracting.
2. Merge its **BepInEx/** folder into the folder containing `Risk of Rain 2.exe`, or into your mod manager's active profile. Replace each mod's two DLLs together.
3. The included `BepInEx/config` files reset these mods to their defaults. **Skip that folder to keep your settings and disc paths**; new options are added automatically on launch.
4. Each mod reads its models, textures and sound from **your own disc images** on first launch: Super Mario Sunshine for Mario, Pikmin for Olimar (plus Pikmin 2 for Louie, Purple and White Pikmin, Candypop Buds and Ship Repair's Pikmin 2 content), and Super Smash Bros. Melee NTSC-U revision 2 for Melee. Set `Paths.Disc`, or use the shared folder above. Existing caches are reused and topped up.

Requires the **RoR2 DLC4 (2026)** line, Windows x64, BepInEx 5.4.21.0 and the dependencies in the included guides. Mario uses native ABI 34, Olimar ABI 52 and Melee ABI 5.

## Before you play

- Everyone in a friends-only co-op game needs these exact builds: **Mario 0.4.7, Olimar 0.4.3 and Melee 0.4.6**. Mario's and Olimar's network formats changed.
- These changes have automated checks, **not a playtest**. None of the new skins, Candypop Buds, Pikmin 2 enemies or treasures, Yoshi, the nozzle wheel or the Melee punches has been played in RoR2 yet.
- Offline and friends-only. No disc images, extracted game assets, game DLLs or dependency DLLs are included. Compare the download with the attached `.sha256` file to verify it.

## Mario 0.4.5, Melee 0.4.3 and Olimar 0.4.1 (`release-20261025`)
### Mario: Luigi, the statue, and a safer landing

#### 0.4.5 notes

- **Luigi is here!** Pick him as Mario's alt-skin: Luigi's own model, green cap particles, his own FLUUD (leaning to the spray side so it clears his head), a pitched-up voice, a higher jump, and his name and quip in the pause sheet. He uses every one of Mario's moves.
- **Unlock Luigi with 2401 coins.** Hold at least 2401 coins (the gold the HUD's coin counter shows) at one time as Mario. Until then the loadout shows the lock and the line "L is real 2401". It is the counter itself, not a running total: spend the coins back down and the HUD has to reach 2401 again.
- **Luigi's effects are green.** The spin jump's swirl spark, the ground pound's smear and the super ground pound's fireball turn their red parts green; white cores and blue highlights stay.
- **The Pianta statue arrival.** On the first stage Mario's pod is the Sunshine Pianta statue with the animated M portal: the arrival sting, the portal's own exit sound, the particle reveal, the spin descent and the landing. The descent fire burns at the pedestal base, and the portal frames are pre-rendered with smoother edges.
- **No more landing underground.** The statue's exit clip ends at the last pose above the ground, so the rider is never drawn with his feet in the dirt and is never set down under the terrain; if the exit point is buried, he steps out onto clear ground.
- **Recovery while pinned.** Being pushed back by the collision guard no longer clears the fall and stuck timers, so a body trapped against a collider, or let out under the map, still gets rescued.
- FLUUD's pack pumps while the Spray fires out of a dive or belly slide.

#### 0.4.0 notes

- **Keep spraying through dives and slides.** With Spray selected, FLUDD now works during a B dive and belly slide. Jumping out of a slide lets you press B to dive again.
- **Quicker water feedback.** Spray water travels 20% faster while keeping its curved, continuous stream, damage and water use; measured range stays within 1% of the previous stream.
- **More reliable FLUDD movement.** Extra Rocket and Hover uses granted by items can be spent again in the same jump. Backup Magazine sets Air Pump's available stock correctly.
- **Finish your ground pound.** Hits no longer interrupt the pound or its landing recovery.
- **Clearer aiming and controls.** Free spray aim avoids Mario's own colliders and stays closer to the camera direction. The pause sheet shows current pad and keyboard controls, with the ship-part row only in Ship Repair.
- **New skill portraits.** FLUDD, nozzles, Ground Pound and Wet Slide have portraits made from your extracted game models, generated on first use and cached for later starts.
- Goobo Mario aims his spray at his target, and Mario now keeps his own death animation instead of the inherited Commando ragdoll.

### Melee: items, eggs and lightning

#### 0.4.3 notes

- **Items work in Melee.** Wax Quail boosts a sprinting jump, H3AD-5T v2 gives the airborne slam and its landing blast (jump in the air with no jumps left), and RoR2's attack speed speeds up move playback. Each has an `[Items]` setting (`WaxQuail`, `Headstompers`, `AttackSpeed`; 1 is RoR2's, 0 turns it off).
- **Egg Lay works on every enemy again.** Yoshi's and Pikachu's Egg Lay reads the target's hurtbox before its body, so enemies with a separate hurt box are edible again.
- **Pikachu's Thunder matches the disc again.** The source's 0x24C cloud is back, the clap uses the extracted sample, the extra Jolt flash is gone, and a Thunder Jolt no longer vanishes from a map-zone test; its arc is drawn along its path.
- **Quick Attack can chain its two charges again.** Its recovery is short enough to spend the second charge in the same hop.
- **Smaller fixes.** Yoshi's own jaw-tongue no longer shows beside the drawn lash, a charging fighter no longer catches on terrain, and ground-only hits reach a laid egg.
- Egg Lay is now a shared service, so other characters can use their own version of it.

#### 0.4.0 notes

**Samus, Fox, Luigi and Yoshi are the most complete Melee fighters.** Pikachu, Captain Falcon, Link and Donkey Kong are still in progress.

- **Samus: bomb, roll and keep moving.** Morph Ball Bomb combines a startup bomb with a held roll, jumping and enemy bounces. Its armor absorbs damage up to 25% of maximum health; release or armor break returns her to normal. Charge Shot can stay held through a jump, full charge hits harder, and shots and missiles travel faster with improved camera aiming.
- **Fox: steer and extend your specials.** Hold Illusion and Fire Fox to extend their movement, steer Illusion with the stick, and follow uphill ground more reliably. Illusion has blue afterimages; Fire Fox ends with a fire impact and clears its launch flame.
- **Luigi: bring a Super Scope.** Choose it as the Fireball alternate, tap for rapid fire or hold a charge, and move or jump while charging. It has visible charge feedback, muzzle effects and ground smoke. Fireball placement, grounded Super Jump Punch and Cyclone behavior also improve.
- **Yoshi: catch, swallow and bowl through enemies.** The tongue follows the crosshair, reaches farther and stops at terrain. Catch several enemies and lay their eggs one at a time; eggs fall and can be knocked around. Egg Roll shows the rolling egg, hits repeatedly, and ends on release, armor break or its time limit.
- **Pikachu: more useful movement and lightning.** Quick Attack follows the camera/stick direction and has two independent charges; another press can queue the next charge. Thunder Shock travels level before arcing, with one blue arc per bounce. Thunder has a cloud, sound and a clearer yellow bolt, plus a close electric burst; overhead cover blocks it. Egg Lay is available as an alternate.
- **More readable skills and controls.** Updated pause sheets show binding glyphs. A attacks move to the B face button; down specials and Shield use LT. Skill descriptions are shorter, and native-model skill portraits are generated and cached from your disc.
- All fighters can cancel a special fall or airborne hit-fall into an A attack. Goobo Jr. clones can use each fighter's own moves and gummy appearance. Fighters keep their own death presentation, and Donkey Kong's tie stays in its intended pose.

### Olimar: unchanged at 0.4.1

#### 0.4.1 notes

- **Less network traffic with large squads.** Pikmin snapshots are smaller, targeting the heavy update traffic when a squad grows past 100 Pikmin. Multiplayer performance still needs an in-game check.
- **Lock onto the job at hand.** Aim at pellets, corpses and ship parts as well as enemies. The free cursor follows the camera and can aim much closer to Olimar.
- **A more useful Onion.** The hovering Onion offers its beam menu, and arrival placement gives it more room beside Olimar and the Dolphin.
- **A clearer Ship Repair field.** Parts show their in-game names; once Pikmin carry a part, its carrier count gets the space. Pikmin and carried parts route around the landed Dolphin, and carriers animate at their carrying pace.
- **More deliberate squad attacks.** Dismiss strikes send Pikmin jumping onto enemies ahead, and attached Pikmin stay closer to the enemy's visible body.
- Corpses come from Olimar, Pikmin and allied minion kills by default; their pellet color follows the Pikmin that made the kill.
- **New skill portraits and clearer controls.** Pluck, Throw, Whistle, Onion and Strike have cached native-model portraits. Sprouts sit properly in the soil, Onion flight sparkles are closer to the source, and Olimar keeps his own fallen pose at game over.
