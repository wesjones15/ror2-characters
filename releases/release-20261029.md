# Mario 0.4.7, Olimar 0.4.3 and Melee 0.4.6

Everything that has changed in each mod since 0.4.0, one mod at a time, newest version first. Each mod's top version is new in this release.

## Mario

### 0.4.7 (new in this release)

- **Play as Yoshi.** Defeat Mithrix's final form while playing Mario to unlock him (you don't have to be alive or land the last hit). He runs on his own legs, flutters when you press A again in the air, and sprays FLUDD's water from his mouth in his juice colour.
- **Pick your nozzle from a wheel.** Tap RB to swap between Spray and your second nozzle; hold RB for half a second to choose that nozzle from a wheel: Hover, Rocket, Turbo or No FLUDD. On keyboard the Special key does the same, with the mouse picking.
- **No FLUDD? Punch.** With FLUDD off, RB and RT throw Melee Mario's attacks with his voice: jab, tilts, smashes and dash attack on the ground, aerials in the air. Needs the Melee mod installed with its cache built.
- **Fire is a threat, and water is the answer.** Catching fire sends Mario hopping with his Sunshine burn voice and flames. A pool, your own spray or another Mario's puts him out, and a wet Mario can't catch fire for a moment.
- **FLUDD fights fire.** Water does 5x damage to burning enemies, fire elites, wisps and Magma Worms, and spraying a fire trail puts that stretch out.
- **Sunshine's heat haze** shimmers over distant scenery (`Look.HeatHaze` turns it off).
- **Your HUD stays when you die.** Coins, life meter and tank gauge stay up while you spectate or fly a Remote Operation drone.
- **Smoother on rough ground.** No more sinking into slopes or getting stuck in rocks (a wedged Mario is freed after 2 s). He steps up small lips instead of stuttering, keeps his speed on ramps, and can start a wet slide over dry ground.

### 0.4.6

- **Piantissimo.** Unlock him by beating the stage-four teleporter event as a living Mario. His Sunshine model, jump voice and pink/brown pound effects; his other lines use Mario's voice pitched down (`Sound.PiantissimoGameVoice` for his game-exact voice only).
- **Catching fire** plays Sunshine's burning reaction, with each skin's hot face.
- **Faster Spray:** it flies 1.4 times as fast as Sunshine's (was 1.2).
- **Statue arrival fixes:** the statue comes straight down to its landing point and its fire sits in the right place.
- Effect textures and the coin pickup's sparkle draw as on the disc.

### 0.4.5

- **Luigi.** Unlock him by holding at least 2401 coins at once as Mario (the HUD's coin counter, not a running total; the locked loadout shows "L is real 2401"). His own model, his own FLUUD leaning clear of his head, a pitched-up voice, a higher jump, and his name and quip in the pause sheet. His spin, pound and super pound effects burn green.
- **The Pianta statue arrival.** On the first stage Mario arrives on the Sunshine Pianta statue through its animated M portal, with its sounds, particle reveal, spin descent and landing fire.
- **No more landing underground**, and a Mario pinned against a collider or dropped under the map is still rescued.
- FLUUD's pack pumps while Spray fires out of a dive or belly slide.

### 0.4.0

- **Spray through dives and slides.** FLUDD keeps spraying during a B dive and belly slide, and jumping out of a slide lets you dive again.
- **Quicker water:** Spray travels 20% faster with the same curved stream, range and damage.
- **More reliable FLUDD movement.** Extra Rocket and Hover uses from items can be spent in the same jump, and Backup Magazine sets Air Pump's stock correctly.
- **Finish your ground pound.** Hits no longer interrupt the pound or its landing.
- **Clearer aiming and controls.** Free spray aim avoids Mario's own colliders and stays closer to the camera direction; the pause sheet shows current pad and keyboard controls.
- **Skill portraits** for FLUDD, the nozzles, Ground Pound and Wet Slide, made from your disc's models.
- Goobo Mario aims his spray at his target, and Mario keeps his own death animation.

## Olimar

### 0.4.3 (new in this release)

- **Three new skins**, each unlocked once on your profile: **Pianta** after stage 1's teleporter as Olimar (from the Mario mod's Sunshine files), **Captain Pikachu** with Ice Climbers for Pikmin after stage 2's (from Melee), and **Louie** with his Hocotate Ship for recovering all 30 ship parts in one Ship Repair run (needs a Pikmin 2 disc).
- **Purple and White Pikmin.** Purples pound where they land and knock fliers out of the sky; Whites are fast and poison whatever eats them. Every Onion holds all five colours, and Louie's squad starts with Purples and Whites.
- **Candypop Buds on every map** (with a Pikmin 2 disc). Throw Pikmin in, and every 5 that go in come straight back out as 5 live Pikmin of the bud's colour, landing beside it in your squad. A bud takes 25 before it withers; the rainbow Pop gives 5 for 1. Lock onto buds like enemies.
- **Weigh fliers down.** 5 Pikmin hanging on a small flier bring it to the ground, 15 on a large one, 25 on a boss.
- **Ship Repair goes Pikmin 2** (with a Pikmin 2 disc): about thirty more enemies, from Bulborb Larvae and Snagrets to the Empress Bulblax, the Segmented Crawbster and the Long Legs, and Pikmin 2 **treasures** around each stage for a separate treasure score, in co-op and the race.
- **Smoother on slopes.** Olimar and his Pikmin no longer hop off downhills or restart their run when sprinting over hills, and anyone wedged in geometry for 2 s is freed.

### 0.4.1 and 0.4.2

- No gameplay changes (rebuilt with the shared code the other mods changed).

### 0.4.0

- **Less network traffic with big squads** (over 100 Pikmin); still needs an in-game check.
- **Lock onto the job at hand:** pellets, corpses and ship parts as well as enemies. The free cursor follows the camera and can aim much closer to Olimar.
- **A more useful Onion.** The hovering Onion offers its beam menu, and it lands with more room beside Olimar and the Dolphin.
- **A clearer Ship Repair field.** Parts show their in-game names and carrier counts, Pikmin and parts route around the landed Dolphin, and carriers animate at their carrying pace.
- **More deliberate squad attacks.** Dismiss strikes send Pikmin jumping onto enemies ahead, and latched Pikmin stay close to the enemy's visible body.
- **Corpses** come from Olimar's, his Pikmin's and allied minions' kills, and their pellet colour follows the Pikmin that made the kill.
- **Skill portraits** for Pluck, Throw, Whistle, Onion and Strike from your disc's models. Sprouts sit in the soil, Onion sparkles match the game, and Olimar keeps his own fallen pose at game over.

- **Luigi's Super Scope charge** uses the disc's own flash, ground smoke, recoil dust and camera kick.
- **Effects:** mirrored particle sprites draw whole, effect models start their own dust and sparks, ground dust sits on the floor, and the falloff recovery flash plays on the returning fighter.
- Egg Lay plays its grab sound on every lash, and Captain Falcon's Kick flame follows his foot.

### 0.4.4

- No fighter changes (rebuilt with shared code; safer extraction).

### 0.4.3

- **Items work in Melee.** Wax Quail boosts a sprinting jump, H3AD-5T v2 gives the airborne slam and landing blast (jump with no jumps left), and attack speed speeds up moves. Each has an `[Items]` setting.
- **Egg Lay works on every enemy again.**
- **Pikachu's Thunder matches the disc again**, and Quick Attack can chain its two charges in one hop.

### 0.4.0

- **Samus: bomb, roll and keep moving.** Morph Ball Bomb combines a startup bomb with a held roll, jumps and enemy bounces, with armor up to 25% of max health. Charge Shot stays held through a jump, a full charge hits harder, and shots and missiles fly faster.

- **Controls:** A attacks are on the B face button, and down specials and Shield use LT. The pause sheets show binding glyphs, and skill portraits come from your disc.
- Every fighter can cancel a special fall or airborne hit-fall into an A attack. Goobo Jr. clones use each fighter's own moves in the gummy material, fighters keep their own death presentation, and Donkey Kong's tie stays put.

## All three: one folder for your discs (new)

The ZIP now includes `BepInEx/plugins/gamecube_library.txt`. It is only notes until you write a folder's path into it. Then put your disc images in that folder, under any names: each mod finds its own game by reading the discs. A mod that hasn't extracted yet builds its files in `assets\Mario`, `assets\Olimar` or `assets\Melee` inside that folder. **Caches you already have stay where they are and keep working.** The mods remember the folder, so a later update that replaces the file keeps it.

## Install or upgrade

1. Download **release-20261029.zip**. On Windows, right-click it, open Properties and tick **Unblock** before extracting.
2. Merge its **BepInEx/** folder into the folder containing `Risk of Rain 2.exe`, or into your mod manager's active profile. Replace each mod's two DLLs together.
3. The included `BepInEx/config` files reset these mods to their defaults. **Skip that folder to keep your settings and disc paths**; new options are added automatically on launch.
4. Each mod reads its models, textures and sound from **your own disc images** on first launch: Super Mario Sunshine for Mario, Pikmin for Olimar (plus Pikmin 2 for Louie, Purple and White Pikmin, Candypop Buds and Ship Repair's Pikmin 2 content), and Super Smash Bros. Melee NTSC-U revision 2 for Melee. Set `Paths.Disc`, or use the shared folder above. Existing caches are reused and topped up.
5. Upgrading from the old standalone Samus 0.2.x mod? Remove `BepInEx/plugins/MeleeSamus` first.

Requires the **RoR2 DLC4 (2026)** line, Windows x64, BepInEx 5.4.21.0 and the dependencies in the included guides. Mario uses native ABI 34, Olimar ABI 52 and Melee ABI 5.

## Before you play

- Everyone in a friends-only co-op game needs these exact builds: **Mario 0.4.7, Olimar 0.4.3 and Melee 0.4.6**. Mario's and Olimar's network formats changed.
- These changes have automated checks, **not a playtest**. None of the 0.4.x changes has been played in RoR2 yet; Melee multiplayer still needs a two-machine test.
- Offline and friends-only. No disc images, extracted game assets, game DLLs or dependency DLLs are included. Compare the download with the attached `.sha256` file to verify it.
