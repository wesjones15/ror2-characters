# Mario, Olimar and Melee 0.4.0

One download, three mods: smoother FLUDD movement, a more useful Pikmin squad, and substantial updates to the Melee fighters.

**Samus, Fox, Luigi and Yoshi are the most complete Melee fighters.** Pikachu, Captain Falcon, Link and Donkey Kong are still in progress.

All three mods now use version **0.4.0**. Changes below are since the previous release, `hotfix-20261022-no-runtime-needed`.

## Mario: keep the water and movement flowing

- **Keep spraying through dives and slides.** With Spray selected, FLUDD now works during a B dive and belly slide. Jumping out of a slide lets you press B to dive again.
- **Quicker water feedback.** Spray water travels 20% faster while keeping its curved, continuous stream, damage and water use; measured range stays within 1% of the previous stream.
- **More reliable FLUDD movement.** Extra Rocket and Hover uses granted by items can be spent again in the same jump. Backup Magazine sets Air Pump's available stock correctly.
- **Finish your ground pound.** Hits no longer interrupt the pound or its landing recovery.
- **Clearer aiming and controls.** Free spray aim avoids Mario's own colliders and stays closer to the camera direction. The pause sheet shows current pad and keyboard controls, with the ship-part row only in Ship Repair.
- **New skill portraits.** FLUDD, nozzles, Ground Pound and Wet Slide have portraits made from your extracted game models, generated on first use and cached for later starts.
- Goobo Mario aims his spray at his target, and Mario now keeps his own death animation instead of the inherited Commando ragdoll.

## Olimar: better squad handling and a clearer battlefield

- **Less network traffic with large squads.** Pikmin snapshots are smaller, targeting the heavy update traffic when a squad grows past 100 Pikmin. Multiplayer performance still needs an in-game check.
- **Lock onto the job at hand.** Aim at pellets, corpses and ship parts as well as enemies. The free cursor follows the camera and can aim much closer to Olimar.
- **A more useful Onion.** The hovering Onion offers its beam menu, and arrival placement gives it more room beside Olimar and the Dolphin.
- **A clearer Ship Repair field.** Parts show their in-game names; once Pikmin carry a part, its carrier count gets the space. Pikmin and carried parts route around the landed Dolphin, and carriers animate at their carrying pace.
- **More deliberate squad attacks.** Dismiss strikes send Pikmin jumping onto enemies ahead, and attached Pikmin stay closer to the enemy's visible body.
- Corpses come from Olimar, Pikmin and allied minion kills by default; their pellet color follows the Pikmin that made the kill.
- **New skill portraits and clearer controls.** Pluck, Throw, Whistle, Onion and Strike have cached native-model portraits. Sprouts sit properly in the soil, Onion flight sparkles are closer to the source, and Olimar keeps his own fallen pose at game over.

## Melee: new ways to move, fight and choose your loadout

**Samus, Fox, Luigi and Yoshi are the most complete Melee fighters.** Pikachu, Captain Falcon, Link and Donkey Kong are still in progress.

- **Samus: bomb, roll and keep moving.** Morph Ball Bomb combines a startup bomb with a held roll, jumping and enemy bounces. Its armor absorbs damage up to 25% of maximum health; release or armor break returns her to normal. Charge Shot can stay held through a jump, full charge hits harder, and shots and missiles travel faster with improved camera aiming.
- **Fox: steer and extend your specials.** Hold Illusion and Fire Fox to extend their movement, steer Illusion with the stick, and follow uphill ground more reliably. Illusion has blue afterimages; Fire Fox ends with a fire impact and clears its launch flame.
- **Luigi: bring a Super Scope.** Choose it as the Fireball alternate, tap for rapid fire or hold a charge, and move or jump while charging. It has visible charge feedback, muzzle effects and ground smoke. Fireball placement, grounded Super Jump Punch and Cyclone behavior also improve.
- **Yoshi: catch, swallow and bowl through enemies.** The tongue follows the crosshair, reaches farther and stops at terrain. Catch several enemies and lay their eggs one at a time; eggs fall and can be knocked around. Egg Roll shows the rolling egg, hits repeatedly, and ends on release, armor break or its time limit.
- **Pikachu: more useful movement and lightning.** Quick Attack follows the camera/stick direction and has two independent charges; another press can queue the next charge. Thunder Shock travels level before arcing, with one blue arc per bounce. Thunder has a cloud, sound and a clearer yellow bolt, plus a close electric burst; overhead cover blocks it. Egg Lay is available as an alternate.
- **More readable skills and controls.** Updated pause sheets show binding glyphs. A attacks move to the B face button; down specials and Shield use LT. Skill descriptions are shorter, and native-model skill portraits are generated and cached from your disc.
- All fighters can cancel a special fall or airborne hit-fall into an A attack. Goobo Jr. clones can use each fighter's own moves and gummy appearance. Fighters keep their own death presentation, and Donkey Kong's tie stays in its intended pose.

## Install or upgrade

1. Download **release-20261024.zip**. On Windows, right-click the ZIP, open Properties and tick **Unblock** before extracting.
2. Merge its **BepInEx/** folder into the folder containing `Risk of Rain 2.exe`, or into your mod manager's active profile. It contains all three mods under `BepInEx/plugins/MarioTest`, `OlimarTest` and `Melee`. Replace each mod's two DLLs together.
3. The included `BepInEx/config` files reset these mods to their defaults. **Skip that folder to keep your settings and disc paths**, or back up your settings and re-enter `Paths.Disc` after replacing them. New options are added automatically on launch.
4. Each mod reads its models, textures and sound from **your own disc image** on first launch: Super Mario Sunshine for Mario, Pikmin for Olimar, and Super Smash Bros. Melee NTSC-U revision 2 for Melee. Set `Paths.Disc` in the mod's config if needed. Existing extracted caches are reused; missing files and skill portraits are generated when needed.
5. If upgrading from the old standalone Samus 0.2.x mod, remove `BepInEx/plugins/MeleeSamus` before installing Melee.

Requires the **RoR2 DLC4 (2026) game compatibility line**, Windows x64, BepInEx 5.4.21.0 and the RoR2BepInExPack dependencies described in the included guides. Dependency mods are installed separately. The native DLLs continue to include their C runtime, so there is no separate Visual C++ runtime requirement.

## Before you play

- Use the same **0.4.0** mod builds on every machine in a friends-only co-op session. Older builds are incompatible with the updated native interfaces and Pikmin networking.
- Samus, Fox, Luigi and Yoshi are the most complete Melee fighters; the other four still have unfinished moves, effects or tuning. This release does not claim the whole roster is finished.
- New gameplay changes have automated/headless checks, **not a release playtest**. Melee multiplayer still needs a two-machine playtest. Large-squad network improvements also need in-game verification.
- The obsolete Mario crouch test has been removed. Melee's extraction safeguard now checks ignored cache folders in-process, without requiring Git to launch, while refusing unignored source folders.

The ZIP also includes the player guides, this changelog, and `release.json` with source revisions and file checksums. No disc images, extracted game assets, game DLLs or dependency DLLs are included. Compare the download with the attached `.sha256` file if you want to verify it.
