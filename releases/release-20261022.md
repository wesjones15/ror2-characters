## Install

1. **Before unzipping, right-click the ZIP, choose Properties and tick Unblock** (or run `Unblock-File` on it in PowerShell),
   so Windows does not warn about the DLLs.
2. Merge the ZIP's `BepInEx` folder into your game folder (the one with `Risk of Rain 2.exe`), or into your mod manager's
   profile. Replace both DLLs of each mod.
3. **New: `BepInEx/config` holds each mod's settings at this release's defaults.** Merging it **resets your settings for
   these three mods**, including `Paths.Disc` and `AssetsDir` if you set them (re-enter those afterwards). To keep your own
   settings, skip the `config` folder; any new setting is then added at its default the first time the game starts.
4. The guides are in `docs/`: `Mario.md`, `Olimar.md` and `Melee.md`.

## What's new

Only the changes since the previous release (Mario 0.3.9, Olimar 0.3.15, Melee 0.3.2); details in `CHANGELOG.md`.

**Melee 0.3.3** (from the first play-test; unplayed):
- Chained attacks (combo hits, the rapid jab, a move right after another) no longer hit for nothing. This is the likely
  cause of Fox dealing no damage.
- Fox's Blaster and Yoshi's Egg Throw deal damage (they hit for nothing). Blaster: every tap fires, holding fires 6 shots
  a second.
- GAME! plays for every fighter, not only Samus.
- Thunder Jolt aims at the crosshair and bounces again (0.3.2 made it crawl).
- Donkey Kong idles and walks in his own pose, not the carry pose. Luigi's up and down smash use their own animations.
- One short pause-menu page per fighter, like Olimar's and Mario's; the text no longer shrinks. The fifth skill icon sits
  in the skill row.
- New `Debug/Diagnostics` setting (on by default) logs `[MeleeDiag]` lines for moves, hits and GAME!. Include them in bug
  reports.

**Mario 0.3.10:** Mario leads the character select except in Ship Repair; the Shine Sprite hangs above his head so he jumps
into it; FLUDD aims toward the camera when the target is behind him; LT crouch and the L-button camera aim are removed;
FLUDD's fallback spray pitch is fixed.

**Olimar 0.3.17:** Pikmin that fall off the map come back to their captain unharmed instead of being killed 60 m down,
including ones that were carrying, latched to an enemy or flattened (they let go first). Also the character select order
(Olimar first in Ship Repair, Mario first elsewhere). Tested headless only; not played.

## Melee: known broken

From the 2026-10-22 play-test, still open in this release:

- **Everyone except Samus:** the Z grab does not catch enemies, and close-range repeat attacks do not stunlock.
- **Fox:** Fire Fox's flame stays after the move, and in testing it had no fire, knockback or stagger. Blaster shots are
  drawn as white boxes, and the Blaster does not fire while moving. No KO light when he is knocked out.
- **Luigi:** extra Cyclone presses queue up and then do nothing. Super Jump Punch (up-B) does not start on the ground.
- **Captain Falcon:** Falcon Kick's fire is not on his leg and flickers. Falcon Punch has no forward drive. Raptor Boost
  (up-B) is weak. His character-select move has no sound.
- **Link:** the boomerang is drawn vertical. The bow is invisible and fires a bomb instead of an arrow. He cannot jump while
  drawing. The hookshot has no model. The bomb uses the wrong model and can only be pulled out or dropped, not thrown.
- **Pikachu:** Quick Attack has no effect and zips once when there is no target. The Thunder Jolt bolt does not animate.
  Thunder has no sound, leaves square artifacts, and its bolt has a black outline.
- **Yoshi:** the tongue looks short and does not catch or eat enemies, so Egg Lay never happens. Egg Roll's egg is not
  drawn, the roll cannot be cancelled, and its damage felt negligible.
- **Donkey Kong:** his tie goes through his head. The grab does not pick enemies up or carry them. Spinning Kong does not
  keep him in the air (and on the select screen it neither spins nor plays sound). Headbutt buries instead of stunning.
  Giant Punch's effects fly off and linger. His health is low for his size.
