# Mario 0.3.8, Olimar 0.3.15 and Melee 0.3.1 (Risk of Rain 2 DLC4)

Three mods in one ZIP, for Risk of Rain 2 **DLC4 (2026)** with BepInEx 5.4.21.0 and RoR2BepInExPack 1.46.0 already installed.
Offline and friends-only co-op only. Everyone in a multiplayer game needs the same Mario and Olimar builds.

| Mod | Version | Folder in `BepInEx/plugins` | Native ABI | Your disc |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mario | 0.3.8 | `MarioTest` (`MarioTest.dll`, `sms.dll`) | 29 | Super Mario Sunshine |
| Olimar | 0.3.15 | `OlimarTest` (`OlimarTest.dll`, `pikmin.dll`) | 44 | Pikmin |
| Melee | 0.3.1 | `Melee` (`Samus.dll`, `ssbm.dll`); five of its eight fighters are a first pass | 5 | Super Smash Bros. Melee, NTSC-U rev 2 |

> **Melee is not finished.** **Samus, Fox and Luigi** are the most complete. **Pikachu, Captain Falcon, Link, Yoshi and Donkey Kong are a first pass: they are not complete and not ready.** Their attacks still have to be finished and tuned, and other per-character details are missing or rough, so expect wrong, missing or placeholder moves, hitboxes, effects and numbers. They are included so they can be tried and so feedback can shape them, not as finished characters.

> **Mario is now a reward.** With the Olimar plugin installed, Mario is locked until you win **Ship Repair as Olimar**. Without Olimar installed he is available from the start.

## Install

1. **Before unzipping, right-click the ZIP, choose Properties and tick Unblock** (or run `Unblock-File` on it in PowerShell).
   Windows marks every file you download, and a marked ZIP marks all its contents, so copying the DLLs into the game
   would otherwise show "These files might be harmful to your computer". Clicking OK on that prompt is also safe; it is the
   standard downloaded-file notice, not a problem found in the DLLs. The DLLs in this ZIP carry no such mark themselves.
2. Merge the ZIP's `BepInEx` folder into your game folder (the one with `Risk of Rain 2.exe`), or into your mod manager's
   profile. Copy only the folders for the mods you want; install each mod's two DLLs together, and replace both DLLs of a
   mod you already have (Olimar's multiplayer messages changed, so everyone in a game needs the new build).
3. Keep the discs out of the ZIP: launch once, set `Paths.Disc` in each mod's config file (or put the disc image beside its
   DLLs; for Melee its filename must contain "Melee"), and launch again. Extraction runs once, on your machine. **Olimar tops up
   his extracted enemy files from your disc on the first launch of this version.**
4. The guides are in `docs/`: `Mario.md`, `Olimar.md` and `Melee.md` (install, controls, configuration, limits).

Upgrading from release-20261020 needs nothing else: the folders, configs and caches are unchanged. (From Samus 0.2.x, delete
`BepInEx/plugins/MeleeSamus/` first, as the Melee guide says.)

## What's new

Only the changes since each mod's last published version (Mario 0.3.7, Olimar 0.3.14, Melee 0.3.0); see `CHANGELOG.md`.

**Melee 0.3.1**: every fighter's specials are now finished as the disc's own move chains, plus **jab 3 and the rapid jab**
(every press and release of A counts; Fox, Captain Falcon and Link have the rapid jab, Luigi, Falcon and Link a third jab),
**voices** (smash grunts, hurt cries scaled by the size of the hit, the KO voice), hit sparks for every fighter, effects for
Captain Falcon, Link and Donkey Kong, Link's sword trail, faces, colour glows and a rim glow (`Visuals.RimLight`), a
generic enemy grab, a `Sim.SpeedScale` setting and generated controls windows.
Per fighter: **Fox**'s Reflector reflects projectiles; **Luigi**'s Green Missile charges and can misfire, his Super Jump
Punch lands with lag and his Cyclone pulls enemies in; **Pikachu**'s Thunder Shock crawls along surfaces; **Captain
Falcon**'s Raptor Boost, Dive and Kick follow the clips; **Link**'s bow charges, the boomerang returns and the bomb is carried;
**Yoshi** flutters, lays eggs (bosses too), rolls steerably and throws arced eggs; **Donkey Kong**'s Giant Punch stores its
swings, his Headbutt buries and his Hand Slap goes on with shockwaves.

**Olimar 0.3.15**: **every Pikmin 1 enemy** from your disc now runs the original game's own AI inside a RoR2 body: Bulborbs
and Bulbears, Wollywogs, the Cannon Beetle and its boulders, Blowhogs, the Snitchbug, Sheargrubs, the Wogpole, Puffstool,
Mamuta (its punch buries), Smoky Progg and Giant Eggs, the Water Dumple, Honeywisps, the Breadbug, Fire Geysers, Pellet
Posies and the Pearly Clamclamp, with director weights and costs in the `[Enemies]` config. **Olimar leads the character
select**, and winning Ship Repair as him unlocks Mario.

**Mario 0.3.8**: Mario sits after Olimar in the character select and is locked until Ship Repair is won as Olimar (when the
Olimar plugin is installed).

## Checks and known limits

- The libsms, libpikmin and libssbm test suites, the managed Mario, Olimar and shared-code checks, and Melee's plugin startup
  and native-boundary checks pass on these sources (`release.json` pins every source commit).
- **None of this was played in game before release.** That includes every Pikmin enemy's movement, effects and sounds, the
  Melee voices, jab chain, glows and effects, and the new specials of the first-pass fighters; see the limits in
  `docs/Olimar.md` and `docs/Melee.md`. Melee does not support multiplayer: a client controlling a Melee survivor falls back
  to Commando.
- The ZIP contains no disc images, extracted game assets, generated audio, game DLLs or dependency DLLs. Unofficial fan project,
  not affiliated with Nintendo, HAL Laboratory, Hopoo Games or Gearbox. Do not share your extracted `assets/` folders.
- Verify the download against the `.sha256` file on the release page; `release.json` lists the SHA-256 of every file inside.
