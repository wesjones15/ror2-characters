# Mario 0.7.0, Olimar 0.7.0 and Melee 0.6.0 - 2026-11-10

For Risk of Rain 2 **DLC4 (2026)** with BepInEx 5.4.21.0 and RoR2BepInExPack 1.46.0. Offline and friends-only co-op
only. Changes since release-20261106 (Mario 0.6.0, Olimar 0.6.0, Melee 0.5.0). Two earlier candidate builds
(release-20261107 and release-20261108, 0.6.1 to 0.6.9) were never published; their changes are in this release.

**None of this has been played in RoR2 yet.** The simulation and the automated checks pass; how it plays and feels
is untested. Expect rough edges, especially the new FLUDD moves and the experimental aimed nozzles.

## Updating from release-20261106

Replace the `wesleyasaurus-Mario`, `wesleyasaurus-Olimar` and `wesleyasaurus-Melee` folders in `BepInEx/plugins`
with the ones in this ZIP. The folders keep their names, so nothing needs deleting this time. Your discs and settings
stay where they are. If you are updating from anything older than release-20261106, do that release's update steps
first (old `MarioTest`, `OlimarTest` and `Melee` folders deleted; discs in
`wesleyasaurus-SharedFiles/put_games_here/`).

**Multiplayer:** everyone needs the same builds (Mario's native interface is ABI 57, Olimar's ABI 66).

## Mario 0.7.0

- New FLUDD moves, each with its own switch in the config (`Sim` section; false is the game's behavior):
  Turbo thrust without feet on the ground (jump, dive, belly slide, corkscrew) and a Turbo sled that flies off
  embankment crests; a spinning ground pound that falls faster; the Rocket and Turbo in a B dive and belly slide,
  with a sustained Rocket sled.
- Experimental aimed Rocket, Turbo and Hover (`Experimental` section, on by default, needs an LB lock-on target):
  a precision Rocket shot (the head aims through the charge-up), an aimed Turbo stream and aimed Hover jets.
- Stand-in Mario (no Sunshine files): Spray head, Rocket smoke, a lying-down pose, and the Lemurian's fireball flames
  for the super pound and burning corkscrew. FLUDD's lock-on leads moving enemies.

## Olimar 0.7.0

- Olimar, Louie and Ship Repair run without Pikmin game files, with stand-in captains and Pikmin, stock drop pod and
  Pikmin 2 bonuses. Stand-in cursor, pellets and fallback HUD counts follow the real simulation.
- Thrown Pikmin keep their 3D arrival guidance, lead a locked target and have damped homing; a pad throw keeps its
  speed after scraping a cliff. Pellet Posies always spawn bloomed.

## Melee 0.6.0

- Fighters, their simulation and procedural views run without game files: Samus's whole kit is authored for it,
  Luigi's Super Scope is built in, and arrivals use a generic entry platform. Locked shots lead a moving body.

The per-mod changelogs list every change.
