# Mario 0.8.0, Olimar 0.8.0 and Melee 0.6.1 - 2026-11-11

For Risk of Rain 2 **DLC4 (2026)** with BepInEx 5.4.21.0 and RoR2BepInExPack 1.46.0. Offline and friends-only co-op
only. Changes since release-20261110 (Mario 0.7.0, Olimar 0.7.0, Melee 0.6.0).

**None of this has been played in RoR2 yet.** The simulation and the automated checks pass; how it plays and feels
is untested. The most important thing to check is multiplayer: a second player with Sunshine's files still being
prepared (a first launch) used to fail to spawn into the stage.

## Updating from release-20261110

Replace the `wesleyasaurus-Mario`, `wesleyasaurus-Olimar` and `wesleyasaurus-Melee` folders in `BepInEx/plugins`
with the ones in this ZIP. The folders keep their names, so nothing needs deleting. Your discs and settings stay
where they are. If you are updating from anything older than release-20261106, do that release's update steps first
(old `MarioTest`, `OlimarTest` and `Melee` folders deleted; discs in `wesleyasaurus-SharedFiles/put_games_here/`).

**Multiplayer:** everyone needs the same builds (Mario's native interface is ABI 57, Olimar's ABI 66).
**Ship Repair now needs the Pikmin and Pikmin 2 discs (see below).**

## Mario 0.8.0

- **Multiplayer fix:** the statue pod and its states now register on every machine. Before, a player whose files were still being prepared
  at launch never got them: the host's pod could not spawn for him, the game's state numbers were shifted, and his log filled with errors.
- The first stage's entrance is the same everywhere: without ready Sunshine files Mario enters under a stand-in statue (gray block of the
  statue's size with a blue disc), and a notice says the files are still being prepared. Nothing is blocked.
- Aimed Rocket: five homing drops at 4 times the Spray's speed and 20 times its hit. Aimed Turbo: a counter-clockwise ring of homing drops at
  2 times the Spray's speed. The disc's Rocket, Turbo and Hover heads turn to the aim. Aimed Hover follows the LB lock in flight (release LB and
  it goes on) and is a gun on the ground.
- A held Turbo draws the dash cone and plays its sounds in a jump, dive, slide and corkscrew.
- A burn or knock-down in the air allows the Hopoo Feather's jump and a ground pound.
- **New:** B picks up small enemies and B throws them (standing, walking, running, jumping); a thrown enemy hurts what it hits.
- Fixes: an Abyssal Depths spawn reset loop, the life meter at zero, far coin sounds, repeated lobby log lines.

## Olimar 0.8.0

- **Ship Repair needs the Pikmin and Pikmin 2 discs on every machine, the host included** (no stand-in version yet). A player missing a disc
  cannot start it or stay in its lobby or run; one whose files are still being prepared makes the host wait.
- A notice says while the Pikmin files are still being prepared.

## Melee 0.6.1

- A fighter whose files are still being prepared is let out of the arrival after 15 seconds instead of waiting for them. A notice says while the
  Melee files are still being prepared.

The per-mod changelogs list every change.
